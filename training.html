      Controls & practice rules

      Touch, mouse and keyboard. Choose a build card, then tap the gold highlighted site. A drag of more than 8 pixels pans and never spends gold. Cancel clears your choice before selecting an owned building.

      Every build and upgrade costs real practice gold. Only the current lesson's highlighted actions are available. Time stops for instructions and runs for observations. Switching away pauses the lesson; resume it yourself.
