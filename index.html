          THE COMMANDER'S FIELD GUIDE

          How to play

          Build and upgrade your army’s buildings to destroy the enemy citadel; your troops march and fight automatically.

          01

          Build your army

          Use the gold that arrives over time to place troop buildings on green slots; their troops march down the nearest lane.

          02

          Combine troop types

          Protect archers with warriors while wizards hit groups of ground troops. Against towers, bring siege; against gryphons that bypass melee, bring archers.

          03

          Commit to a lane

          Build several troop buildings in one lane, or spread them across lanes. Forward buildings reinforce faster, but are exposed.

          04

          Support your troops

          Towers buy time, but don't heal. Upgrade surviving buildings, invest in gold mines, or use an arrow volley to thin enemy troops.

          A war of attrition. Towers can be worn down by continuous waves. After 5 minutes, production accelerates. Only in timed endgame, both citadels begin to crumble after 7 minutes. Open endgame has no timed citadel decay.

          Mouse: Left-click to place once or select · Shift-click to keep building · Drag or middle mouse drag to pan · Right-click to cancel placement
          Touch: Tap to build once, then choose again · Drag to pan · Tap the minimap to jump

          GAME SETTINGS

          Options

          Changes apply immediately.

          Fullscreen depends on your browser and device. Esc exits fullscreen.

          Battle keyboard controls

          Shortcuts use physical key positions, so the labels may differ on non-US keyboards. Build slots follow the current battle’s roster and earned unlocks.

          Choose Change, then press a letter, digit, numpad digit, arrow or Space. Clear leaves an action unbound. Esc cancels capture; Tab moves on. Esc, Tab, Enter, modifier combinations and system keys are reserved. Only camera panning repeats while held.

          Optional guided practice retains its default controls. These settings apply to campaign, coaching and skirmishes in the main game.

          Controller controls · experimental

          Connect a standard controller and press a button.

          Left stick: cursor · Right stick: pan · A: confirm/place · B: back · X: HUD focus · Y: arrow volley · LB/RB: buildings · D-pad: navigate controls · Start: pause · View: home · Left stick press: follow · LT: precision.

          In menus, use the stick or D-pad to select a control, then press A. Left/right adjusts sliders and choices. Touch and keyboard remain available. Physical controller testing is ongoing.

          Keep this session safe

          This tab will not write over a changed or partially restored profile. Keep it open while recovering. Reload replaces this tab’s retained battle with the verified saved profile. You can export the in-memory session first.

          Save backups

          Download a portable JSON copy. Importing replaces this browser’s campaign stars, trial records, preferences, history and saved battle. Files stay on your device.

          Recent battles

          Preferences, saved battles and medals stay on this browser or device. No account or tracking.
