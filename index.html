      THE COMMANDER'S FIELD GUIDE

      Win the ground war.

      Destroy the enemy citadel. Your buildings raise troops automatically, so your job is the plan.

      01

      Build the engine

      Select a building, then tap a green slot. Troops march down its nearest lane. Gold flows in over time.

      02

      Make a mixed army

      Warriors screen your archers. Wizards punish ground swarms. Gryphons bypass melee; archers shoot them down. Siege breaks towers.

      03

      Commit to a lane

      Stack producers behind a push or spread out to divide the enemy. Forward buildings reinforce faster, but are exposed.

      04

      Turn the tide

      Towers buy time, but don't heal. Upgrade surviving buildings, invest in Gold mines, or use a Rally strike to thin enemy troops.

      A war of attrition. Towers can be worn down by continuous waves. After 5 minutes, production accelerates. After 7, both citadels begin to crumble.

      Touch: Tap to build · Drag to pan · Tap the minimap to jump
      Keyboard: 1–7 buildings · R strike · Space pause · Esc cancel · Arrow keys pan

      Controller · experimental: Left stick moves the cursor, right stick pans. A confirms, B cancels, LB/RB chooses buildings, X opens HUD focus, Y arms Rally, Start pauses. D-pad navigates controls. LT slows the cursor. Connect a standard controller and press a button. Physical hardware validation is ongoing.
